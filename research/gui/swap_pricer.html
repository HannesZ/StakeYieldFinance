How to read: The fair fixed rate is the model-expected average staking APR over the swap period. A swap buyer locks in this rate (minus protocol spread) instead of receiving the variable staking yield. If staking APR falls below the fixed rate, the buyer profits.

Note: Model uncertainty (p5–p95) is narrow because the consensus-layer yield formula is deterministic given total staked ETH. Real uncertainty from MEV variability, protocol changes, or issuance reform is not captured.
